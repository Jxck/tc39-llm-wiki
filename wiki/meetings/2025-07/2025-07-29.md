## How to make thenables safer?

- wiki: [Thenable Curtailment](../../proposals/thenable-curtailment.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-thenable-curtailment)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1_RCnI7dzyA1COgi_Ib7GkmHioV1nVSEMmZvy0bvJ8Kk/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)

[MAG](../../people/MAG.md) (Matthew Gaudet) asked the committee for a direction of defense against the security problem caused by treating an object that has a `then` property as a thenable. The options considered were using a new internal slot such as `[[InternalProto]]` to stop the search for a thenable from a built-in prototype, and inserting an extra microtick into Promise resolution. Strong opposition came from [JRL](../../people/JRL.md) and [KG](../../people/KG.md) that an extra tick is unacceptable for performance. [WH](../../people/WH.md) and [MAG](../../people/MAG.md) supported not recognizing a thenable on a built-in prototype, and [KG](../../people/KG.md) also leaned toward the `[[InternalProto]]` plan. Between [MAG](../../people/MAG.md) and [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) it was pointed out that two problems are mixed: a new object unintentionally becoming a thenable, and safely resolving a Promise from arbitrary user code. The discussion ran out of time without a conclusion, and continued later.

## Error.captureStackTrace

- wiki: [Error.captureStackTrace](../../proposals/error-capture-stack-trace.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-error-capturestacktrace)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1RGNDcJee_6N2II0SeGyMVglvjhJbuqDX02LLzBqyCWI/)

[DLM](../../people/DLM.md) (Dan Minor) proposed standardizing `Error.captureStackTrace`, which comes from Chrome and is widely implemented, and asked the committee whether to take the accessor style (lazy) or the property style (eager) that V8 had pointed out in an issue. [OFR](../../people/OFR.md) (Google) was concerned about a performance regression from computing a property immediately. [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) and [MM](../../people/MM.md) pointed out that an accessor may be needed for consistency with a future customization mechanism such as `prepareStackTrace`. Opinion also split on supporting arbitrary objects other than Error, and on whether to add an internal slot. [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) said an overly complicated mechanism should not be introduced for a feature that should not have existed in the first place. Conclusion: There was no decision between an accessor and a data property. The discussion was taken to TG3.

## Non-extensible Applies to Private

- wiki: [Non-extensible Applies to Private](../../proposals/nonextensible-applies-to-private.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-nonextensible-applies-to-private)
- slide: [link](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-nonextensible-applies-to-private/blob/main/no-stamping-talks/non-extensible-applies-to-private-update.pdf)

[MM](../../people/MM.md) (Mark Miller) presented a Stage 2.7 status update. For the proposal that forbids adding a private field to a non-extensible object, he explained fixed-shape optimization of structs (motivated by [SYG](../../people/SYG.md)) and a use in Agoric's virtual-object memory system. A temporary rise showed up in Google's compatibility statistics, and the cause turned out to be Babel's transpile output (a `return` override pattern). [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) presented a new future-proof transform, and [WH](../../people/WH.md) also supported that new approach. Conclusion: Toward a Stage 3 request at the next meeting, confirming the statistics with Google and writing and approving a test262 test plan are needed, and it was confirmed that no other additional work is required.

## Immutable `ArrayBuffer` for stage 3

- wiki: [Immutable ArrayBuffers](../../proposals/immutable-arraybuffer.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-immutable-arraybuffer)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/18JnyoJsovfw7Y_HGa0cZOOUCHY2MTO4_M72zKj7PUWo/edit?usp=sharing)

[RGN](../../people/RGN.md) (Richard Gibson) reported that the test262 test plan is nearly finished, and asked to advance to Stage 3. [OFR](../../people/OFR.md) raised a concern that more detaches of `ArrayBuffer` would slow all access through a V8 global flag. [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [KG](../../people/KG.md) treated that as acceptable, because if worker use grows in the future then detaches will grow too. [KG](../../people/KG.md) also pointed out that on the WebIDL side there is a default of not accepting an immutable `ArrayBuffer` (the same problem as a resizable `ArrayBuffer`), and that was treated as part of the Stage 3 work. Conclusion: Consensus for Stage 3 was reached, conditional on merging the test262 PR.

## Iterator Chunking for Stage 2.7

- wiki: [Iterator Chunking](../../proposals/iterator-chunking.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-iterator-chunking)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/17qDtY-2Qawt7SeKoY7Rezea-A_hAuwhx2QHJ9MCZ7as)

[MF](../../people/MF.md) (Michael Ficarra) presented a plan with two methods, `windows` and `sliding` (they differ when the trailing elements are shorter than the window size). A strong view came from [KG](../../people/KG.md), [SHS](../../people/SHS.md), [JHD](../../people/JHD.md), and others that "two methods are hard to tell apart, and one method with a string parameter is better." The committee agreed to proceed with a single method and a string parameter, but the names of the parameter values (`discard` / `truncate`, and others) were not settled, and discussion continues on the issue tracker. Conclusion: It was decided to proceed with one method that takes a string parameter, but the concrete value names are unsettled. After discussion on the issue tracker, Stage 2.7 will be aimed at again at the next meeting.

## [Keep trailing zeros in `Intl.NumberFormat` and Intl.PluralRules](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros) for Stage 2 or 2.7

- wiki: [Intl Keep Trailing Zeros](../../proposals/intl-keep-trailing-zeros.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-keep-trailing-zeros)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hKJFrDfiGeqPWm51fQFQb4M4CeYm3ultB7Opef1BVuE/edit?usp=sharing)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) (Eemeli Aro) presented a spec change that, as a bugfix, keeps the trailing zeros of a number given as a string, which `Intl.NumberFormat` / `Intl.PluralRules` had been discarding. The public API does not change. Only internal processing such as `ToRawPrecision` / `ToRawFixed` changes. With support from [SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md), Stage 2 was approved. For Stage 2.7, [WH](../../people/WH.md) held it for the moment because of a concern about `ToIntlMathematicalValue` (discussed in the [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) proposal below), but [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) finished their reviews as reviewers during the meeting. Conclusion: Stage 2 was obtained. The continued discussion on Day Three also reached Stage 2.7.

## [~~Measure~~Amount](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-measure) for Stage 2

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-measure)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1my6X1ODDckzJmtcWcFI9hRF_I06Z4RQwrq81lbo8wPM/edit?usp=sharing)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) renamed the proposal "[Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)," with a reduced scope that drops compound units and unit conversion, and asked for Stage 2. An Amount is an immutable object that holds a mathematical value, a precision, and an arbitrary unit identifier, and it provides `toBigInt()` / `toNumber()` / `toString()` / `toLocaleString()`. [KG](../../people/KG.md) questioned whether it is needed as a language feature (is a userland library enough?). [MM](../../people/MM.md) and [KG](../../people/KG.md) argued strongly that the `Intl.NumberFormat` side should not read an Amount internal slot directly and should use property access. The largest issue was the treatment of non-finite values (Infinity / NaN) that [WH](../../people/WH.md) pointed out. The current spec throws from the constructor, and [WH](../../people/WH.md) strongly opposed that as contrary to a norm of the language (any Number / BigInt ought to be representable as output) and called it a blocking concern. Conclusion: The proposal was renamed Amount, but it did not advance to Stage 2 while [WH](../../people/WH.md)'s concern about non-finite values was unresolved. Each issue (rounding behavior, the policy of not special-casing currency, and minimum / maximum limits) continues on the issue tracker.
