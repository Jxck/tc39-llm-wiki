## Restricting subclassing of built-ins

- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-rm-builtin-subclassing)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1J0xct8EHUC90P6QpggISxuAp_5L_qaDa3SZ9gQaB0kA/edit#slide=id.p)

[YSV](../../people/YSV.md) presented Mozilla telemetry: subclassing of built-ins appears in up to ~26% of page loads, dominated by core-js. The committee reached consensus to remove subclassing support (the species-style kind) from TypedArray, ArrayBuffer, and SharedArrayBuffer, with RegExp possibly investigated later. Going forward, new built-ins would support only the simpler type-1 subclassing; species (type 3) cannot be removed from existing built-ins.

The conclusion was consensus for restricting subclassing on TypedArray/ArrayBuffer/SharedArrayBuffer.
