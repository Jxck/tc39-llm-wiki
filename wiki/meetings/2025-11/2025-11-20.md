# 2025-11-20 (Day Three)

## Intl Era Monthcode for Stage 3

- wiki: [Intl Era/Month Code](../../proposals/intl-era-month-code.md)

A proposal that sorts out "era" and "monthCode" for calendar systems other than the Gregorian solar calendar. Two normative changes were presented, and both were approved. Advancement to Stage 3 was deferred until the January 2026 meeting, to leave more review time.

Conclusion: the two normative changes were approved, and advancement to Stage 3 carries over to the January 2026 meeting.

## Amount Stage 1 update

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)

A Stage 1 progress report on the `Amount` proposal, which attaches a unit to a number. The direction shown was to pull the scope back from a general value representation, which had been under discussion, toward unit conversion. The committee reacted favorably: narrowing the scope makes the design easier to see.

Conclusion: no request to advance. It continues at Stage 1, with the change of direction.

## Intl Energy Units for Stage 1

A proposal to add energy units (joule, watt-hour, and others) to the unit list of `Intl.NumberFormat`. Displaying electricity consumption was given as a use case, and there were questions about consistency with the existing unit list.

Conclusion: advancement to Stage 1 was approved.

## `Object.getNonIndexStringProperties` for Stage 1 or 2

A proposal for `Object.getNonIndexStringProperties`, which on an array-like object returns only the string properties that are not index keys. Use cases were explained (enumerating extra properties of a sparse array, and others), and the committee supported the concept.

Conclusion: advancement to Stage 1 was approved.

## Decorators Status Update Request

- wiki: [Decorators](../../proposals/decorators.md)

A report on the implementation status of the [Decorators](../../proposals/decorators.md) proposal (currently Stage 2.7). Insufficient test262 coverage was pointed out, and it was shared that V8, JavaScriptCore, and SpiderMonkey do not agree on an implementation plan and are in a stalemate. The committee asked that tests and coordination across implementations come first.

Conclusion: no request to advance. It stays at Stage 2.7, and the gaps in test coverage and cross-implementation coordination were confirmed.

## Class composition: Past, present, and future

A presentation that organizes the history of composition patterns in class syntax, and the direction from here. The point was background for later related proposals such as Class spread syntax and Class field introspection. No particular stage decision was made.

## Class spread syntax for Stage 1

A proposal to introduce, in a class definition, a pattern like object spread (`{...obj}`). Chip Morningstar ([CM](../../people/CM.md)) and Waldemar Horwat ([WH](../../people/WH.md)) raised design concerns (interaction with existing class-field and method syntax becomes complicated), and there was no agreement.

Conclusion: advancement to Stage 1 was not approved. It continues at Stage 0.

## Class field introspection for Stage 1

A proposal to reflectively obtain and inspect a class's field definitions. Mark S. Miller ([MM](../../people/MM.md)), Gus Caplan ([GCL](../../people/GCL.md)), and James M Snell ([JSL](../../people/JSL.md)) raised concerns about security and encapsulation, and agreement was not reached.

Conclusion: advancement to Stage 1 was not approved. It continues at Stage 0.

## Continuation: Declarations in Conditionals for Stage 2

Continued discussion of a syntax extension, not settled on Day One, that allows a variable declaration inside a condition. Syntactic ambiguity and the scope rules were discussed further, and this meeting did not reach agreement either.

Conclusion: no agreement on Stage 2. Discussion continues.

## Continuation: Composites comparator choice

Continued discussion of the comparator choice for Composites from Day Two. Building on the Day Two TCQ temperature checks, which supported interning and throwing when inserting into a leaked WeakMap, the committee discussed how to reflect that in future spec text. There was no formal request to advance a stage. The session continues to refine the design.
