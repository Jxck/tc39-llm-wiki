## Normative: fully define Math.sqrt

- proposal: [ecma262#3345](https://github.com/tc39/ecma262/pull/3345)

[MF](../../people/MF.md) and DGN presented fully specifying `Math.sqrt`'s result instead of leaving it implementation-approximated. All engines already produce bit-identical results over 10 million sampled inputs (the underlying sqrt instruction is correctly rounded), so the change ratifies reality at no implementation cost, with [WH](../../people/WH.md) supportive.

The conclusion was consensus on the PR.

## unordered async iterator helpers for Stage 1

- wiki: [Unordered async iterator helpers](../../proposals/unordered-async-iterator-helpers.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/michaelficarra/proposal-unordered-async-iterator-helpers)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1EDhoV4Vyh1Pte-W2qWvvCeLwhQ61dMFT55GNg0VeDLM)

[MF](../../people/MF.md) proposed splitting unordered consumption out of async iterator helpers as `AsyncIterator.prototype.unordered()` plus a `UnorderedAsyncIterator.prototype`, letting concurrent async tasks pull from one iterator without sequencing guarantees. [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) worried about designing too far ahead of async iterator helpers itself; [MF](../../people/MF.md) committed not to seek Stage 2 until async iterator helpers advances and there is field experience. [MM](../../people/MM.md) asked to avoid double coercion.

The conclusion was Stage 1.

## Error.isError for Stage 2.7

- wiki: [Error.isError](../../proposals/error-is-error.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-is-error)

[JHD](../../people/JHD.md) did not ultimately ask for Stage 2.7 — spec review is still outstanding — but presented the PR removing proxy piercing from the brand check. The committee agreed on the PR, noting it "does not constitute a precedent for checking or not checking internal slots on arguments (not the receiver) of static methods". [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) offered a litmus: platform errors that act like built-in JS errors (e.g. `DOMException`) should return true.

The conclusion was no advancement — the proposal returns next meeting for Stage 2.7.

## Temporal update & bug fixes

- wiki: [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-temporal)
- slide: [link](http://ptomato.name/talks/tc39-2024-07/)

[PFC](../../people/PFC.md) reported [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) is nearly done and shipping unflagged, with Test262 coverage at SpiderMonkey 96%, V8/LibJS 75%, JSC 40%, Boa 23%. The valueOf/toJSON collapsing idea was dropped, and "Stop Coercing Things" was explicitly not applied — no change reaffirmed. Two normative fixes reached consensus: handling the TZDB Toronto 1919-03-31 23:30 DST start-of-day corner case, and throwing RangeError for `Duration.round` when roundingIncrement is over 1 with a calendar smallestUnit and largestUnit different from smallestUnit.

The conclusion was consensus on the two normative fixes; the proposal proceeds toward Stage 4.

## Normative: Add text about locale installation in browser implementations as fingerprinting vector

- proposal: [ecma402#780](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/780)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/fingerprinting-slides#/)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) presented ECMA-402 text warning implementers that which locales are installed can be a fingerprinting vector. No consensus formed on the wording: [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) argued the wrong stakeholders are in the room and browser privacy teams need to weigh in on whether such text is necessary, and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) will check with [YSV](../../people/YSV.md).

The conclusion was to reach out to the relevant stakeholders, including privacy teams, on both the language and its necessity.
