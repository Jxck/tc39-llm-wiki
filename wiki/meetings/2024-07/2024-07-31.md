## Propagate active ScriptOrModule with JobCallback Record

- proposal: [ecma262#3195](https://github.com/tc39/ecma262/pull/3195)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1FQNSpCdzkvcRg-yFBjUOqjrNAVHezfoVLjgK4cfEjIc/edit#slide=id.p)

[CZW](../../people/CZW.md) presented the mismatch between ECMA-262 and HTML over when a promise job's ActiveScriptOrModule is captured: ECMA-262 captures it at `HostEnqueuePromiseJob` (revealing `[[PromiseState]]` through the referrer used by e.g. dynamic import), while HTML captures it at `HostMakeJobCallback`. Chrome, Firefox, and Safari each behave differently across the cases. [KKL](../../people/KKL.md) and [MM](../../people/MM.md) asked to settle the related indirect-eval question first.

The conclusion was to revisit the topic later in the day, after the eval discussion.

## Decimal for Stage 2

- wiki: [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-decimal)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/proposal-decimal-tc39-july-2024)

[WH](../../people/WH.md) and [DE](../../people/DE.md) preferred `equals`/`lessThan` etc. to return booleans rather than tri-state values (NaN handling), with `compare` for the tri-state; [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [WH](../../people/WH.md) flagged incoherence in the `toFixed`-style option-bag signatures. The conclusion was no advancement — [JHD](../../people/JHD.md)'s block stands and discussion continues offline.

## Avoid capturing lexical context in indirect eval

- wiki: [Avoid capturing lexical context in indirect eval](../../proposals/avoid-capturing-lexical-context.md)
- proposal: [ecma262#3374](https://github.com/tc39/ecma262/pull/3374)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Xko1Md81wXpUFvgH_nQVl0-DW9hyqlOTkZX3y7pImvg/edit)

[NRO](../../people/NRO.md) presented the corner case where dynamic import inside indirect eval resolves its specifier against the calling module's referrer — accidental dynamic scoping and a refactoring hazard, since indirect eval is otherwise just a function. Web reality is split: Chrome and Firefox follow ECMA-262, Safari does something else. With implementability and priority concerns from [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md), the change was converted from a normative PR into a Stage 2.7 proposal (merge gated on implementations), at [KM](../../people/KM.md)'s suggested process.

The conclusion was Stage 2.7, with a proposal repo to be created.

## Updates from TypeScript: deferred and immediate

- slide: [link](https://onedrive.live.com/?authkey=%21ANzaoMgiLDZwOCw&id=5D3264BDC1CB4F5B%216170&cid=5D3264BDC1CB4F5B&parId=root&parQt=sharedby&o=OneUp)

[DRR](../../people/DRR.md) (Microsoft) raised awareness of TypeScript-only syntax under exploration — not a JS proposal — to improve control-flow analysis through callbacks. `deferred` (annotating callbacks that do not run immediately) was tried in the 5.6 beta and dropped as requiring too many annotations; `immediate` (marking parameters whose callbacks may run synchronously, e.g. the array methods) is now favored as incrementally adoptable, though it might also lead to doing nothing. [WH](../../people/WH.md) preferred `immediate`; [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) and [DRR](../../people/DRR.md) converged that the annotation is a property of the receiving parameter, not the callback.

The conclusion was no ask — an informational update with feedback welcome.
