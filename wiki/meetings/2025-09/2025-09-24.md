## Increase limits on Intl MV

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1V1BC6PtJ7-q6zVvsgKt9dcaLmIeeLnE3s8DwP4KOl7Q/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)

Presenter: Shane F Carr ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md))

A proposal for a PR that raises the exponent and significant-digit limits on string-derived values of an Intl mathematical value (IntlMV), looking ahead to a future Decimal128 proposal. Today there is in effect a limit equivalent to 408 digits. Two plans were compared: an exponent of ±10,000 with a significant-digit cap of 500, versus 20,000 (in effect limited by 10,000 fraction digits). [WH](../../people/WH.md) pointed out that the name "mathematical value" and the limit contradict each other, while agreeing with the presented exponent limit of ±10,000.

### Conclusion

Two-step consensus: (1) agreement to extend the IntlMV limits in preparation for Decimal128, (2) agreement on the concrete limits as the slide's "currently proposed solution" (exponent ±10,000, and a limit based on fraction digits rather than a significant-digit cap of 500).

## Amount continuation

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/2025-09-tc39-plenary-amount-continuation#/)

Presenter: Ben Allen ([BAN](../../people/BAN.md))

A wrap-up of the [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) discussion of this day (and of the days before). The way significant digits are computed was treated as resolved by [NRO](../../people/NRO.md)'s PR, but the name (whether [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) or another name) and whether numeric conversion methods (`toNumber` / `toBigInt`) are right stayed unresolved and carry to the next meeting (Tokyo). [KG](../../people/KG.md) asked that "the reason it exists as a class, not only as a protocol" be written down again. [WH](../../people/WH.md) replied that removing the conversion methods damages the interoperability purpose itself. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) summarized that there is a degree of agreement on Intl / MessageFormat use, but delegates are split on whether to include uses other than Intl.

### Conclusion

- As of today (2025-09-24), [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) **has not reached Stage 2**. Two unresolved issues remain, the name and the treatment of the numeric conversion methods. The champions continue the work offline and will present it again at the next Tokyo plenary.
