## Continuation: Amount for Stage 2

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-amount)

Continuation of the discussion from the previous days. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) of Google / V8 presented five concerns about [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) (a back door for decimal, limiting it to formatting use, that it is not a general solution, referring to an internal slot, and consistency with the HTML amount tag), and said that for Stage 2 they agree with the motivation for Intl use, but resolving these is a condition. [JHD](../../people/JHD.md), [WH](../../people/WH.md), [KG](../../people/KG.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md), and others argued hard about the name (`LabelledNumber`, `PlainAmount`, and others) and about whether numeric conversion methods (`toNumber` / `toBigInt`) are right, and there was no conclusion. [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) proposed a way to compute significant digits (including the treatment of 0), and [WH](../../people/WH.md) agreed. Conclusion: a continuation was requested; at this point it did not advance to Stage 2, and discussion continues.

## Increase limits on Intl MV

- proposal: [PR #1022](https://github.com/tc39/ecma402/pull/1022)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1V1BC6PtJ7-q6zVvsgKt9dcaLmIeeLnE3s8DwP4KOl7Q/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (Shane F Carr) presented a PR that raises the exponent and significant-digit limits on string-derived values of an Intl mathematical value (IntlMV), looking ahead to a future Decimal128 proposal. Today there is in effect a limit equivalent to 408 digits. Two plans were compared: an exponent of ±10,000 with a significant-digit cap of 500, versus 20,000 (in effect limited by 10,000 fraction digits). [WH](../../people/WH.md) pointed out that the name "mathematical value" and the limit contradict each other, while agreeing with the presented exponent limit of ±10,000. Conclusion: two-step consensus: (1) agreement to extend the IntlMV limits in preparation for Decimal128, (2) agreement on the concrete limits as the slide's "currently proposed solution" (exponent ±10,000, and a limit based on fraction digits rather than a significant-digit cap of 500).

## Continuation: Update on proposal-module-global

- wiki: [Module Global](../../proposals/module-global.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/endojs/proposal-module-global)
- slide: [link](https://github.com/endojs/proposal-module-global/blob/main/slides/2025-09-stage1-update.pdf)

[KKL](../../people/KKL.md) (Kris Kowal) presented continued Q&A on the module-global proposal (Stage 1), which introduces a new global scope by a compartment. [KM](../../people/KM.md) and [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) (SpiderMonkey) and others raised concerns about implementation complexity and performance. [KKL](../../people/KKL.md), [MAH](../../people/MAH.md), and [MM](../../people/MM.md) explained that the combination of a compartment and lockdown becomes the base of supply-chain-attack defense. Also discussed were the relationship to [ShadowRealm](../../proposals/shadowrealm.md), the efficiency of a near membrane across a callable boundary (a proxy gets in the way of JIT optimization), and the need to avoid transpilation of the module source. [MM](../../people/MM.md) raised concern about the sharp rise in supply-chain attacks in the LLM era, and [WH](../../people/WH.md) agreed. There was no separate stage-transition decision (the conclusion is in the original 2025-09-23 topic); the proposal stays at Stage 1, collecting feedback.

## Amount continuation

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)
- proposal: [Amount](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-amount)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/2025-09-tc39-plenary-amount-continuation#/)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) (Ben Allen) gave a wrap-up of the [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md) discussion of this day (and of the days before). The way significant digits are computed was treated as resolved by [NRO](../../people/NRO.md)'s PR, but the name (whether Amount or another name) and whether numeric conversion methods (`toNumber` / `toBigInt`) are right stayed unresolved and carry to the next meeting (Tokyo). [KG](../../people/KG.md) asked that "the reason it exists as a class, not only as a protocol" be written down again. [WH](../../people/WH.md) replied that removing the conversion methods damages the interoperability purpose itself. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) summarized that there is a degree of agreement on Intl / MessageFormat use, but delegates are split on whether to include uses other than Intl. Conclusion: as of 2025-09-24, Amount **has not reached Stage 2**. Two unresolved issues remain, the name and the treatment of the numeric conversion methods. The champions continue the work offline and will present it again at the next Tokyo plenary.
