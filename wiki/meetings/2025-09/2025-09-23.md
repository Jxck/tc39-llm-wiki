## Intl Era Month Code Stage 2.7 Update

- wiki: [Intl Era Month Code](../../proposals/intl-era-month-code.md)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/2025-09-tc39-plenary-era-monthcode-update#/)

  [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) reported Stage 2.7 progress. The remaining work is finishing test262, with a plan to aim at Stage 3 at the next plenary. Overflow behavior on non-ISO calendars (especially lunisolar calendars that have a leap month), and how `PlainMonthDay` computes its reference year, were explained as the issues. A revision was shown that widens the reference-year search from 1972 through 2035, and editorial cleanup (rewriting prose into algorithm steps) is also in progress.
  There were no particular questions, and it ended as a progress report.

## Deferred re-exports update

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1ok-qUnKrHK8ADWAHR071t5dglDkuKhXc0mPLCTS0meY)

  [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) reported progress on `export defer`. The spec text is finished. As the counterpart of `import defer`, it defers loading of an unused binding. Interaction with `export * from` (whether every deferred binding should be loaded through a re-export) is an unresolved issue and has been filed.
  Three reviewers, [ACE](../../people/ACE.md), [CZW](../../people/CZW.md), and [GB](../../people/GB.md), were approved, and the review setup toward Stage 2.7 is in place. Browser implementation of `import defer` is in progress, and this proposal is expected to aim at 2.7 alongside that.

## AsyncContext yield\*

- wiki: [AsyncContext](../../proposals/async-context.md)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1g7Xgf9uAxv5gZYvms23m2_hP513IRYNktz0fDiE1NmA)

  [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) proposed a change to how [AsyncContext](../../proposals/async-context.md) propagates in a generator's `yield*`. Today the context is restored on every turn of the `yield*` loop. Changing that to once, after the loop ends, lets the context at the caller's `.next()` call propagate into the inner iterator. That lets userland write a helper that merges a context such as an `AbortSignal`.
  [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) answered [MM](../../people/MM.md)'s checking questions (it does not itself provide combining contexts; it only reifies them), and there was no objection to the change.
  It is a Stage 2 proposal, but it was shared that the spec text is nearly stable and, waiting on web integration, it is close to Stage 2.7.

## Continuation: Intl Era Month Code—Normative changes

- wiki: [Intl Era Month Code](../../proposals/intl-era-month-code.md)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/2025-09-tc39-plenary-era-monthcode-update)

  [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) asked for formal consensus on spec changes made at the previous plenary. The first is a revert: when adding from a year that has a leap month into a year that does not, and the result is a month that does not exist, drop the previously adopted behavior of "advance to the next non-leap month" and return to throwing a `RangeError` when `overflow: "reject"` is specified. The second extends the `MonthDay` reference-year search from "1972 and earlier only" through 2035, which is needed to handle correctly a rare leap month (M11L) that appears in the Chinese calendar in 2033 / 2034.
  Support of +1 came from [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [ACE](../../people/ACE.md), and both changes got the committee's consensus.

## Update on proposal-await-dictionary

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1TaLCZt2jJtrVY1PjFd49jlBW-vAVil4NYsOzAyYPP7Q/)

  [ACE](../../people/ACE.md) reintroduced `Promise.allKeyed`, which takes a named object and returns results under the corresponding names, as an alternative to `Promise.all` (the naming follows `Iterator.zip` / `Iterator.zipKeyed`). The presentation was to take the committee's temperature, not to seek consensus.
  In questions, support gathered from [KG](../../people/KG.md), [ZTZ](../../people/ZTZ.md), and [MM](../../people/MM.md) for adding `Promise.allSettledKeyed`. [MM](../../people/MM.md) also pointed out that `all` and `allSettled` are both needed (neither replaces the other), and the direction was to adopt it as an additional method. After discussion from [MM](../../people/MM.md) and [OFR](../../people/OFR.md), a syntax extension will be considered later as a separate proposal, and will not be included in this one. [JSL](../../people/JSL.md) said "asking for Stage 2.7 is too early," and [ACE](../../people/ACE.md) agreed. [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) and [JSL](../../people/JSL.md) volunteered as reviewers.

## Import Bytes for stage 2.7

- wiki: [Import Bytes](../../proposals/import-bytes.md)
- slide: [link](https://slides-import-bytes-2025-09.vercel.app/)

  [STY](../../people/STY.md) asked for Stage 2.7 for the [Import Bytes](../../proposals/import-bytes.md) proposal (a mechanism that imports an arbitrary byte sequence as a `Uint8Array`, backed by an immutable `ArrayBuffer`, with `type: "bytes"`). Feedback since last time has been reflected, including the change from `type: "buffer"` to `type: "bytes"` and returning a `Uint8Array` rather than an `ArrayBuffer`. The spec text is finished, and review by three reviewers is done.
  Many people supported it, including [MM](../../people/MM.md), [JSL](../../people/JSL.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), and [JHD](../../people/JHD.md). In particular, it was added that a static import can obtain the bytes synchronously, which fetch cannot. It was also confirmed that, because the Immutable ArrayBuffer proposal is currently Stage 2.7 (conditional Stage 3), this proposal has a dependency that it cannot advance past that.
  Consensus for Stage 2.7 was reached.

## Update on proposal-module-global

- slide: [link](https://github.com/endojs/proposal-module-global/blob/main/slides/2025-09-stage1-update.pdf)

  [KKL](../../people/KKL.md) and [ZTZ](../../people/ZTZ.md) reported progress on the Stage 1 module-global (Compartment) proposal. It is a mechanism that creates a new global scope and a module map inside the same realm. The main motivations explained were an isolated environment for tests, supply-chain-attack defense (LavaMoat and others), and a multi-tenant plugin system. Today this is done in userland with a shim that combines a `with` statement, sloppy mode, direct `eval`, and Proxy, but performance and fidelity have limits, and language support is needed.
  It was shared that, taking the previous feedback, a redesign that absorbs ideas from the older Compartment proposal is underway, that a new evaluation path (eval) is not the main purpose, and that a non-serializable hook on the module source will not be added. [KG](../../people/KG.md) raised a strong question about the relationship to [ShadowRealm](../../proposals/shadowrealm.md), and time ran out, so it carried to a later session.

## Temporal update and normative change

- wiki: [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)
- slide: [link](https://ptomato.name/talks/tc39-2025-09/)

  [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) reported progress on [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md). Two implementations (V8 and Boa, both based on `temporal-rs`) now pass 99% of test262, and the Kiesel engine has also added an implementation. The path to Stage 4 was explained as: Intl Era Month Code reaching Stage 3 next time, unflagging the V8 implementation, and moving tests that remain in the `staging` folder into the main suite. A plan was also shown to aim at Stage 4 for three proposals together: Canonicalization, Era Month Code, and this one.
  As a formal normative change, a fix was proposed for a sign-flip bug at a daylight-saving transition, in `ZonedDateTime` difference calculation and in `Duration`'s `round` / `total`. It is a regression from a refactoring about two years ago, prompted by a report from a user (Patrick Hensley). With support from [DLM](../../people/DLM.md), [OFR](../../people/OFR.md), and [JSL](../../people/JSL.md), it reached consensus.

## How Websites are Put Together

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1vEYoTix5yHN3vc1cXZQCk87P8d_GqL0bYsOpoEcnjuo/edit?usp=sharing)

  [KG](../../people/KG.md), in an informational session that did not seek consensus, explained that a large commercial site is built as an uncoordinated collection of scripts from several teams and several companies. Load order cannot be controlled, and patching built-ins continues for the whole lifetime of the page, so mechanisms that assume global coordination (operating CSP script hashes, a "lockdown mode" that freezes every built-in, a single import map, and so on) do not work in practice.
  As a conclusion, [KG](../../people/KG.md) gave a personal view that the committee should spend time on features that can actually be used on this kind of site (usable by one script, with no coordination). [CM](../../people/CM.md) pointed out that this reality is itself a persuasive argument for the Compartment proposal (reducing the need for coordination by isolating modules). Discussion continued with [MAH](../../people/MAH.md), [REK](../../people/REK.md), [JSL](../../people/JSL.md), [WH](../../people/WH.md), and others about how to think about investment in a future generation of applications, but this was not an item that sought consensus, and there is no conclusion.
