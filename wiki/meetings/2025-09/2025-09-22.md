## Convention: strings-as-enums are kebab-case

Kevin Gibbons ([KG](../../people/KG.md)) proposed, for the `how-we-work` conventions document, that a string used as an enum value should be kebab-case. ECMA-262 already has kebab-case examples (`Atomics.wait`, base64, [iterator chunking](../../proposals/iterator-chunking.md)), but ECMA-402 adopted camelCase by a 2019 decision, and those strings are also reused as keys of a property bag. The web platform's design principle recommends kebab-case, and [KG](../../people/KG.md) argued that new APIs should match the web platform. Intl ([SFC](../../people/SFC.md), [EAO](../../people/EAO.md)) supported keeping camelCase, for existing internal consistency, and [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md) is heading toward following camelCase because it reuses Intl's roundingMode. Accepting both, and a compromise of kebab-case for new 262 APIs while Intl stays as it is, were also discussed, but there was no conclusion, and discussion continued on day 3.

## Normative: Add `[[CompactDisplay]]` slot to `Intl.PluralRules`

- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/tg1-normative-PR-1019-sept-2025.md#/)

  Ben Allen ([BAN](../../people/BAN.md)) proposed a normative change that adds a `[[CompactDisplay]]` slot to `Intl.PluralRules`. It is for languages whose plural rules differ between a short compact form ("1K") and a long compact form ("one thousand"). CLDR does not have that data yet, so there is no effect on existing implementations. [KM](../../people/KM.md) asked about the effect on related implementations, and conditional support was given on the view that there is none. Consensus was reached, and it was approved.

## Normative: Make `Intl.PluralRules` ResolvePlural and associated AOs take Intl mathematical values rather than Numbers

- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/tg1-normative-PR-1026-sept-2025.md#/)

  [BAN](../../people/BAN.md) presented a normative proposal that changes `ResolvePlural` / `ResolvePluralRange` to take an Intl mathematical value rather than a Number. That lets `Intl.PluralRules.select` / `selectRange` accept a BigInt. [MM](../../people/MM.md) asked about the terminology mismatch between "Intl mathematical value" and the "numerical value" of the [amount](../../proposals/amount.md) proposal, and it was noted that the name might change when the concept is later lifted into 262. [WH](../../people/WH.md) pointed out that the name "mathematical value" is misleading, but that did not affect the substance of the discussion. Consensus was reached, and it was approved.

## `Array.prototype.pushAll` for Stage 1

- slide: [link](https://danielrosenwasser.github.io/tc39-slides-2025-09-array-push-all)

  Daniel Rosenwasser ([DRR](../../people/DRR.md)) presented, for Stage 1, a proposal to add a new method to `Array.prototype` (provisionally `pushAll` / `pushFrom`) so that a bulk add by spread, such as `array.push(...iterable)`, does not stack-overflow. [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) was skeptical that a huge array is a common case, but [JRL](../../people/JRL.md) and [KG](../../people/KG.md) replied that it is a problem that actually happens often. [MF](../../people/MF.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) also said "handling a huge array is the mistake in the first place" and "the existing `push(...arr)` should be optimized on the implementation side." [KG](../../people/KG.md) pointed out that explicit support from the browsers is needed. In the end the problem statement (safety of stack overflow and performance) was widely supported, and it reached Stage 1 as a generalized problem statement that also includes splice and unshift.

## Normative: change PromiseResolve species check

- slide: [link](https://mhofman.github.io/proposal-native-promise-adoption/slides/2025-09-pr-3689/)

  Mathieu Hofman ([MAH](../../people/MAH.md)) presented a normative PR that changes the `.constructor` lookup inside `PromiseResolve` to a check based on `[[GetPrototypeOf]]`. Today, polluting `Promise.prototype.constructor` can push the internal promise-adoption of `await` back to assimilation through `.then` (this actually happened in Node.js's crypto implementation). This change makes the check unobservable for a native promise. After discussion with [KM](../../people/KM.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), [DLM](../../people/DLM.md), and [OFR](../../people/OFR.md), the judgment was that web-compatibility data has to be collected, and it became "approved in principle (waiting on measurement by the engines)."

## Native Promise Adoption for stage 1

- slide: [link](https://mhofman.github.io/proposal-native-promise-adoption/slides/2025-09-stage-1/)

  [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) made a Stage 1 proposal that, against the problem that promise-prototype pollution can interfere when an async function returns the result of another async call as-is, the resolve function internally adopts the state of a native promise. [JRL](../../people/JRL.md) actually tried to break zone.js with a monkey-patch and could not, reported that zone.js's transpile mechanism through `.then` keeps it safe, and supported it strongly. Support also came from [SHS](../../people/SHS.md), [JHD](../../people/JHD.md), and DM, and it reached Stage 1 with no opposition. Compatibility measurement on the browser side is still needed.

## Native Promise Predicate for stage 1 or 2

- slide: [link](https://mhofman.github.io/proposal-native-promise-predicate/slides/2025-09-stage-1/)

  [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) proposed adding a brand-check predicate that can tell whether something is a native promise without side effects. `Promise.resolve` cannot be used because it has side effects, and `Error.isError` was cited as precedent. On the name, CZW was concerned that `isPromise` encourages misuse with a thenable, and [KG](../../people/KG.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) suggested a longer name such as `isNativePromise`. [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) clearly opposed [ZTZ](../../people/ZTZ.md)'s `isThenable` idea because of a TOCTOU problem. After reaching Stage 1 with no opposition, [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) also asked on the spot to advance to Stage 2. On the premise that the API name is settled during Stage 2, [JHD](../../people/JHD.md), [JSL](../../people/JSL.md), and [JRL](../../people/JRL.md) became reviewers, and it reached Stage 2 as well.
