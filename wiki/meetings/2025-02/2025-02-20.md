## Decision Making through Consensus - take 2

- slide: [link](https://github.com/msaboff/tc39/blob/master/TC39%20Consensus%20take%202.pdf)

[MLS](../../people/MLS.md) (Michael Saboff, Apple) argued that consensus is unanimity in practice — one member withholding is a veto — used mostly by a small group of long-serving members, driving people away and deterring newcomers. He proposed that withholding consensus require 5% of delegates, minimum two. [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) agreed with the diagnosis but defended the process ("no is temporary and yes is forever"); [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) said the change would spread the social cost now borne by lone objectors; [JSL](../../people/JSL.md) wanted blocking to need a second; [DE](../../people/DE.md) stressed ambiguity over whether things were blocked or withdrawn. [MM](../../people/MM.md) defended unanimity as social norms, citing lone dissents that saved the committee ([WH](../../people/WH.md) on SharedArrayBuffer), and warned browsers hold a de facto veto anyway.

The conclusion was to continue the discussion in the afternoon continuation — no process change was proposed for consensus.

## Continuation: A unified vision for measure and decimal

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md), [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md)
- proposal: [measure](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-measure/), [decimal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-decimal/)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1050DHlNOzcN-8LqJQ_6z8j-LryXgEqOcLfcVzkhJyEk/edit#slide=id.p)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) (Shane Carr, Google) returned with the type renamed "[Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)" and a tight scope: an opaque type carrying a numeric value, its precision, and a dimension (currency or unit), with no unit conversion. Motivation: fixing Intl consistency bugs, a prerequisite for the MessageFormat data model. He asked whether decimal (which has spec text) and Amount were ready for Stage 2. Reception of Amount was skeptical: [KG](../../people/KG.md) advocated a protocol-only approach, [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) called it an "Intl NumberFormat options bag factory", [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) urged lighter-weight alternatives, and [MF](../../people/MF.md) warned against limiting it to decimals. [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) were informally added as champions alongside the on-leave [BAN](../../people/BAN.md).

The conclusion, per the speaker's summary: concerns about Amount's motivation if its only use case is Intl; requests to explore a protocol-based approach; an open question on the representation of precision; and no delegate raised concerns about [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md)'s motivation.

## Continuation: `Number.isSafeNumeric`

- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/Lxxyx/proposal-number-is-safe-numeric/issues/4)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Noxi5L0jnikYce1h7X67FnjMUbkBQAcMDNafkM7bF4A/edit)

[LIU](../../people/LIU.md) (ZiJian Liu, Alibaba) returned with a reworked problem statement: report whether a numeric string retains its precision when converted to a Number, motivated by Alibaba's API gateway carrying Java `Long` values as strings and DingTalk Sheets shipping decimal.js everywhere. [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) found the motivation clear at last, [KKL](../../people/KKL.md) reframed it as a family of int64/decimal methods, [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) supported, and [MF](../../people/MF.md) changed his mind once the round-trip problem was articulated. But [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) objected that the safety stops at parsing and he did not want to back-form a problem statement the champion is not solving, and [GCL](../../people/GCL.md) and [MLS](../../people/MLS.md) also held back.

The conclusion was to withhold consensus for Stage 1, with [CDA](../../people/CDA.md) suggesting a unified problem statement be nailed down with all participants before the next plenary.

## Language design goal for consensus: Things should layer

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Nj6E1h0SeyDGI3e8BQlATQeX-l6x4Jx7uGAM8XimfIM/edit#slide=id.g329dc435965_0_344)

[DE](../../people/DE.md) (Daniel Ehrenberg, Bloomberg) proposed a design goal: things should layer — most features should be sugar or libraries over core capabilities, and a new capability should be added only when it is the point of the proposal (e.g. [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)'s `Now`). He argued engines and build tools are similarly constrained, so syntax should desugar locally; [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) and [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) refined how engine and tool constraints differ. [KG](../../people/KG.md) pushed back that new capabilities are the best reason for syntax, so the statement was refactored: features not motivated by a new capability should be expressible via desugaring. [MM](../../people/MM.md) added a preference for more locally desugaring designs.

The conclusion was conditional consensus on the modified statement: when new features include new capabilities, this should be for a particular reason; it is a reference point for future discussions, not a stage-advancement checklist item, with wording to be finalized on GitHub with review from [MM](../../people/MM.md), [SYG](../../people/SYG.md), [KG](../../people/KG.md), and [NRO](../../people/NRO.md).

## Continuation: [Decision Making through Consensus - take 2]

[PFC](../../people/PFC.md) called the "fails safe" framing survivorship bias, since ideas killed by lone vetoes are unknowable. [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) and [JSL](../../people/JSL.md) wanted two blockers from different organizations ([REK](../../people/REK.md) cautioned against defining financial relationships in rules); [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) preferred a flat "two" over 5%. [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) said blocking culture yields "we can live with it" advancements without implementer interest ([decorators](../../proposals/decorators.md), [ShadowRealm](../../proposals/shadowrealm.md)). [JSC](../../people/JSC.md) floated an anonymous temperature check. [MM](../../people/MM.md) moved toward [MLS](../../people/MLS.md): weakening the single veto between Stage 3 and 4, and requiring another member to agree an objection is in good faith. [CM](../../people/CM.md) and AKR were nervous about upending a working process.

The conclusion, per [RPR](../../people/RPR.md)'s chair summary: the committee broadly agrees there are problems to solve, any cure must not be worse than the disease, and the chairs are open to ideas in plenary and outside — no rule change was made.
