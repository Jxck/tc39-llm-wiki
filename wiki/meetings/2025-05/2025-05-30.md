## Comparisons (né Assertions) for Stage 1

- wiki: [Comparisons](../../proposals/comparisons.md)
- proposal: [proposal-comparisons](https://github.com/JakobJingleheimer/proposal-comparisons)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Nr-9OiZUilFlkUxKd4nHbJf3ie736zx335P_VCXVZ18/view)
- slide: [update](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1lMB6kn7wVR5Nreurg6ykZvNf5uprdJzM2IXP9BX4LvM/view)

[JSH](../../people/JSH.md) (Jacob Smith, OpenJS), in his first proposal, asked Stage 1 for [Comparisons](../../proposals/comparisons.md), covering two problems: **A-versus-B** deep comparison (today served by overlapping `assert`/`expect` libraries and needed for validating user input) and **failure output** (usable diffs). [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) urged outreach to testing libraries; [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) argued testing and validation need separate APIs; [MM](../../people/MM.md) refused any piercing of proxy encapsulation and required a problem statement distinct from the mechanism; [WH](../../people/WH.md) found the terminology a "Tower of Babel" and warned production-stripped asserts must never validate user input; [ACE](../../people/ACE.md) found the scope far too big but offered support conditional on rapid splitting; [MF](../../people/MF.md) suggested starting from plain SameValue assertions.

The conclusion, per the speaker's summary: the proposal was split into three — **Inspector** (detailed information about values, also usable by Pattern Matching), **Modes** (conditionally running code, a `NODE_ENV`-like mechanism that also works in browsers), and **Comparisons** itself (assert/expect, likely narrow at first). No stage was granted in this session.

## JS Numerics breakout session

- wiki: [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/tc39-2025-05-decimal-minisummit#/)

[JMN](../../people/JMN.md) ran a decimal mini-summit aiming to clear the path to Stage 2. [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) asked for a concise, pointed README. On construction (`Decimal.from` vs `new Decimal` vs coercing `Decimal()`), a temperature check split 4 for `Decimal.from` versus 6 unconvinced, while preserving space for future decimal primitives drew strong support ([EAO](../../people/EAO.md): "a strong indication we ought to continue with the decimal design in a way that preserves the possibility of primitive decimals"). Most time went to String-backed versus [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md)-backed [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md): [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) favored a numeric-string black box, while [WH](../../people/WH.md) called it "a bug farm" and stressed Amount's value is returning the **rounded** value. A Stage 2 temperature check on Decimal alone was mostly positive, with [MM](../../people/MM.md) uncomfortable if Amount stays attached but "very happy" for decimal alone.

The conclusion was that the champions commit to discussing a String-backed Amount, and there appears to be good support for Decimal going to Stage 2, though with uncertainty surrounding Amount.

## Revisiting Comparison for Stage 1

- wiki: [Comparisons](../../proposals/comparisons.md)
- proposal: [proposal-comparisons](https://github.com/JakobJingleheimer/proposal-comparisons)
- slide: [update](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1lMB6kn7wVR5Nreurg6ykZvNf5uprdJzM2IXP9BX4LvM/view)

[JSH](../../people/JSH.md) returned with the split proposal, asking Stage 1 for **Inspector** only: detailed, human-friendly information about values (e.g. a failed comparison's diff), which is expensive in userland and may need special access to proxies or internal slots. Modes needs more investigation, and [Comparisons](../../proposals/comparisons.md) proper will wait until Inspector and Modes reach Stage 2. [MM](../../people/MM.md) required that **all** special access live behind Modes, since it breaks encapsulation, and then approved; [WH](../../people/WH.md) supported on the same condition; [MF](../../people/MF.md) and [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) asked clarifying questions about scope and host-internal inspection.

The conclusion was that Inspector reached Stage 1 (special access contingent on the forthcoming Modes proposal), with support from JKP and [WH](../../people/WH.md) and [MM](../../people/MM.md)'s qualified approval — the Comparisons proposal itself remains at Stage 0.
