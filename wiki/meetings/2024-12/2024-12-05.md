## Vision for numeric types in ECMAScript

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Uzrf-IwPrljF2BhCbCWuwQxlgGSm_bcd3FRbPO3Yrio/edit#slide=id.p)

[SFC](../../people/SFC.md) framed five numeric problem spaces: decimal arithmetic, representing precision (1 star vs 1.0 stars), more significant digits, unergonomic footguns, and attaching units. Representing decimal _values_ is already possible in `Number`; the problems arise with arithmetic and normalization. He surveyed six solution shapes, from the [Measure](../../proposals/amount.md) proposal and IEEE decimal128 (with or without precision) to `Number.prototype` decimal methods with a tooling operator layer ([RBN](../../people/RBN.md): TypeScript will not type-directed-emit `+`) and a scaled BigInt. [MM](../../people/MM.md) argued trailing-zero precision matches no coherent use case; [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) wanted [Temporal](../../proposals/temporal.md)-style distinct types; [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) held a primitive decimal is the right long-term answer; [JMN](../../people/JMN.md) wanted Measure and [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md) kept separable.

The conclusion was discussion only: number-related champions to keep iterating toward a clean, future-proof architecture across the decimal/measure/precision proposals.

## Measure Stage 1 update

- wiki: [Amount](../../proposals/amount.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39-transfer/proposal-measure)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/17ypyikW1q8RFf5AnnYKpe5dsdrHTb0BnSzZGaq0mm-I/edit?usp=sharing)

[EAO](../../people/EAO.md) (presenting for [BAN](../../people/BAN.md), on medical leave) presented the [Measure](../../proposals/amount.md) strawman: a new primordial pairing a number with a unit/dimension, separating the "what" from the formatting "how", with mixed-unit formatting and unit conversion, which `Intl.NumberFormat` (and `Intl.MessageFormat`) can format directly. Currency conversion and operator overloading are out of scope. Open questions: extra unit data, locale/usage-based conversions (e.g. person-height), arithmetic, and whether Measure should care about anything beyond formatting and conversion. [WH](../../people/WH.md) said precision handling is the long pole and needs a presentation first; [MM](../../people/MM.md) established, with [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) agreeing, that Measure must carry its own precision theory and that no coherent theory tracks only trailing zeros.

The conclusion was no clear committee opinions on the scope questions; further discussion on the representation and handling of precision was requested.

## Continuation: Error Stacks Structure for Stage 2

- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-error-stacks)

[JHD](../../people/JHD.md) split the proposal into three pieces: the normative-optional `stack` accessor, a `getStackString` static, and the structured `getStack`. [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) recalled SpiderMonkey's failed attempt to align stacks with V8 and argued standardizing the schema without contents is **harmful**, spreading non-interoperable stack inspection; [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) agreed for the structure part and said standardizing would not force V8 to change its non-standard behavior. [MM](../../people/MM.md) argued the accessor alone is hugely valuable for censoring (JSC's own data property and V8's own accessor leave no hook) and offered to split it off; [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) said Mozilla would welcome specifying the accessor, which causes web-compat problems today.

The conclusion was a split: a new proposal for just the stack accessor (which became [Error Stack Accessor](../../proposals/error-stack-accessor.md), [MM](../../people/MM.md) co-championing) to be brought back for Stage 1, with the existing proposal refocused on the structure and its README documenting these concerns.

## Continuation: import defer updates

- wiki: [import defer](../../proposals/import-defer.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-defer-import-eval/)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1yFbqn6px5rIwAVjBbXgrYgql1L90tKPTWZq2A5D6f5Q/)

[NRO](../../people/NRO.md) ran the slide-7 temperature check deferred from Day 2 (2024-12-03): should _any_ query about a module's exports (`Object.keys`-style listing, `in` checks), not just `Get`, trigger evaluation? For: it matches what bundlers/transpilers can implement, where most ESM runs. Against: users lose the ability to list exports without evaluating. The poll came back 3 strong positive / 9 positive / 3 following / 1 indifferent; with [GB](../../people/GB.md) absent, [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) read his message asking that such tradeoffs be made openly, then asked for consensus with no objections.

The conclusion was consensus for the key-list evaluation trigger, completing three changes (with hiding `then` and the `"deferred module"` `toStringTag` from Day 2); the `Symbol.evaluated` probe was not asked for, given [Stabilize](../../proposals/stabilize.md) works in the same area.
