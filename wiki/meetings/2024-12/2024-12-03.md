## Briefing on the formation and goals of TC55 (or, All About Moving the WinterCG into Ecma)

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1WnqF7y52QlPRw737ZOTC4rdmJ65-nT9BbOD05jr2sjE/edit?usp=sharing)

[JSL](../../people/JSL.md) (Cloudflare) explained why WinterCG is moving from a W3C community group into Ecma as **TC55**: its "minimum common API" draft drew compliance claims, but a community group cannot publish anything normative. The charter covers the minimum common API (a subset of web platform APIs, verified via a web-platform-tests profile) plus optional conformance levels, working through WHATWG/W3C rather than forking them. [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) asked why not a W3C working group; [LCA](../../people/LCA.md) said consensus landed on Ecma's process. [CM](../../people/CM.md) confirmed coordination with TC53, and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md)/[ABO](../../people/ABO.md)/[MAH](../../people/MAH.md) noted the API set should be a superset of the web's `Exposed=*` set.

The conclusion was an informational briefing: work closely with TC39, TC53, WHATWG etc., and workshop the charter language differentiating TC55 from TC53.

## Stabilize to stage 1

- wiki: [Stabilize](../../proposals/stabilize.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/Agoric/proposal-stabilize)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1474EreKln5bErl-pMUUq2PnX5LRo2Z93jxxGBNbZmco/edit?usp=sharing)

[MM](../../people/MM.md) proposed unbundling integrity levels into orthogonal "integrity traits", motivated by the **return override** mistake (private fields grafted onto existing objects, breaking fixed-shape structs), the **assignment override** mistake (frozen primordials breaking legacy code), and **proxy traps** as an unvalidatable re-entrancy source. The minimal picture keeps non-extensible, adds a `stable` trait (overridable + non-trapping) and a separate `fixed`. [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) supported for structs' sake and floated retconning non-extensible to imply `fixed` (a use counter is deployed); [KG](../../people/KG.md) warned extra object states are conceptually expensive; [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) found the unbundled picture simpler and asked for a glossary.

The conclusion was Stage 1 (explicit support from [SYG](../../people/SYG.md), weak support from [JWK](../../people/JWK.md), plus [JHD](../../people/JHD.md)).

## Module Harmony: where we are

- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1V2-4Hj-HBVQwdphcJUsrbmbitOPBMSf3HhKSvhBk4d0/edit?usp=sharing)

[NRO](../../people/NRO.md) gave the yearly module Harmony overview, with no normative asks: [import attributes](../../proposals/import-attributes.md) at Stage 4, source phase imports at Stage 3, [ESM Phase Imports](../../proposals/esm-phase-imports.md) heading to 2.7, [import defer](../../proposals/import-defer.md) at 2.7. The structural shift: thanks to [GB](../../people/GB.md)'s phase-imports work, **module expressions/declarations no longer depend on a ModuleInstance concept** and become syntax over `ModuleSource`s, so ModuleInstance imports are no longer pursued. He grouped the space into three clusters: ModuleSource (incl. [KKL](../../people/KKL.md)'s "cohorts" for shared-struct prototypes), optional/sync evaluation, and custom loaders/compartments (little progress; help welcome).

The conclusion was a status overview to set common ground for the module presentations later in the meeting.

## Immutable ArrayBuffer to stage 2

- wiki: [Immutable ArrayBuffers](../../proposals/immutable-arraybuffer.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-immutable-arraybuffer)
- slide: [link](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-immutable-arraybuffer/blob/main/immu-arraybuffer-talks/immu-arrayBuffers-stage2.pdf)

[MM](../../people/MM.md) asked for Stage 2 with spec text reviewed by [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) and a full XS implementation. `transferToImmutable` gains an optional length parameter ([WH](../../people/WH.md), [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) concurring; [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) noted expansion may not be zero-copy). [JLS](../../people/JLS.md)'s `ArrayBuffer.prototype.subarray` idea was rejected ([SYG](../../people/SYG.md): "ArrayBuffers are never windows... and TypedArrays are"), and [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) was "on the fence" about `sliceToImmutable` without a concrete motivation. On failure modes [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) wanted alignment with existing detached/out-of-bounds behavior, against [MM](../../people/MM.md)'s "when in doubt, throw"; [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) and [KG](../../people/KG.md) debated the accessor naming.

The conclusion was Stage 2 (support from [WH](../../people/WH.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), [JHD](../../people/JHD.md), [JLS](../../people/JLS.md), [CM](../../people/CM.md)), with order-of-operations and throw-vs-silent details to be settled during Stage 2.

## import defer updates

- wiki: [import defer](../../proposals/import-defer.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-defer-import-eval/)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1yFbqn6px5rIwAVjBbXgrYgql1L90tKPTWZq2A5D6f5Q/)

[NRO](../../people/NRO.md) proposed changes to fix last plenary's problems. (1) To keep the proposal implementable by tools, **any query about the module's exports** (`Object.keys`, `getOwnPropertyNames`, `in`, etc.) would trigger evaluation; [GB](../../people/GB.md) questioned trading away export feature-detection for polyfillability, while [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) sided with designing for today's tools. (2) **Hide `then`** on deferred namespace objects, since `import.defer(...)` promise resolution otherwise always triggers evaluation. (3) A `"deferred module"` `toStringTag` ([JHD](../../people/JHD.md): not a brand check). (4) A `Symbol.evaluated`-style probe got support in principle ([KG](../../people/KG.md): no new well-known symbol; [MM](../../people/MM.md) linked it to [Stabilize](../../proposals/stabilize.md)). Editors' spec review remains the Stage 2.7 condition, with Stage 3 targeted next meeting.

The conclusion was consensus on hiding `then` and the `toStringTag` change; consensus was **not** asked for the key-list trigger (slide 7) given 2-2 mixed feedback, which was deferred to a temperature check in a continuation on Day 4.

## Error Stacks Structure for Stage 2

- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-error-stacks)

[JHD](../../people/JHD.md) revived the error-stacks proposal unchanged since its 2019 Stage 2 attempt: it specifies only the **structure** (a normative-optional `Error.prototype.stack` accessor plus static `getStack`/`getStackString`), leaving frame contents implementation-defined; co-champion [MM](../../people/MM.md) argued an agreed schema beats parsing strings. [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) asked what it improves without specifying the contents; [MAG](../../people/MAG.md) called it "multiple proposals in a trench coat" and urged splitting; [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) registered a blocking SpiderMonkey concern about forcing engines to converge internal representations; [WH](../../people/WH.md) preferred keeping one proposal.

The conclusion was no advancement: the champion plans a new, smaller proposal for just the existing stack accessor (which later became [Error Stack Accessor](../../proposals/error-stack-accessor.md)), with the existing proposal to "rebase" on top of it.
