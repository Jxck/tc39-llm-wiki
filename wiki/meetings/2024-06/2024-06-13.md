## Decimal for Stage 2

- wiki: [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-decimal)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/june-2024-tc39-decimal)

[JMN](../../people/JMN.md) asked for Stage 2 with the spec text considerably fleshed out with [WH](../../people/WH.md)'s help: a Decimal128 data model with decimal NaN/infinities, the five official IEEE 754 rounding modes, and a `compare` that works by mathematical value. [WH](../../people/WH.md) still found significant spec problems (category errors, unimplemented rounding, zero handling) and would support Stage 2 once fixed; a meta-debate followed on what the Stage 2 spec-text bar means ([NRO](../../people/NRO.md), [CDA](../../people/CDA.md), [MF](../../people/MF.md), [RBN](../../people/RBN.md), [DE](../../people/DE.md)). [EAO](../../people/EAO.md) questioned whether the Intl.NumberFormat integration had been directly motivated, and [JHD](../../people/JHD.md) blocked: as an object rather than a primitive the proposal does not carry its weight; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) (Agoric) also withheld consensus given the contention on multiple fronts.

The conclusion was that [Decimal](../../proposals/decimal.md) stays at Stage 1 — not even conditional Stage 2 — with another round of spec iteration and deeper Intl integration (in particular PluralRules) needed.

## ESM Phase Imports

- wiki: [ESM Phase Imports](../../proposals/esm-phase-imports.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-esm-phase-imports)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/17uYZ9-pm2Aa2yw1iP8OsOvRwZBWjgjn6Xid5jxEV7ZE/edit?usp=sharing)

[GB](../../people/GB.md) presented [ESM phase imports](../../proposals/esm-phase-imports.md): importing a module's source phase object, motivated by ergonomic, statically-analyzable worker instantiation, and doubling as the layering primitive for module expressions and module declarations (dynamic import and structured clone of module sources). The proposal also specifies analysis functions (`imports`, named/wildcard exports) for bundlers and wrapper modules; importing a module source from another realm currently throws, with relaxing that and a possible upstream spec refactoring explored in Stage 2. Support was broad ([RGN](../../people/RGN.md), [LCA](../../people/LCA.md), [DE](../../people/DE.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), [CM](../../people/CM.md), [DLM](../../people/DLM.md)).

The conclusion was Stage 2; [GB](../../people/GB.md) will follow up with a README PR on the Stage 2.7 process, and [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) will schedule an overall modules-group presentation for a future meeting.

## Intl.DurationFormat Stage 3 update and normative PRs

- wiki: [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-intl-duration-format)
- slide: [link](https://notes.igalia.com/p/pj5uX_5nC#/)

[BAN](../../people/BAN.md) presented a small TG2-approved normative PR for [Intl.DurationFormat](../../proposals/intl-durationformat.md): in the digital-clock styles (`numeric`/`2-digit`), grouping separators are turned off, so a duration of a million seconds renders `1000000` instead of `1,000,000`, preserving the digital-clock metaphor. [DLM](../../people/DLM.md) and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) expressed support. The proposal is close to asking for Stage 4, with tests for recent normative PRs and an editorial refactor remaining.

The conclusion was consensus on the PR.

[RBN](../../people/RBN.md) returned with [Explicit Resource Management](../../proposals/explicit-resource-management.md) PR #219 (deferred from April pending [NRO](../../people/NRO.md)'s review, which has since landed): an `await using` declaration must Await at least once on block exit, but null/undefined-initialized resources each introduced their own extraneous Await. ## Discard Bindings update or stage 2

- wiki: [Discard bindings](../../proposals/discard-bindings.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://tc39.es/proposal-discard-binding/)
- slide: [link](https://1drv.ms/p/s!AjgWTO11Fk-TkrFz0j1_3aLYU4vABg?e=YMk3IB)

[RBN](../../people/RBN.md) resolved the sole blocking concern from April — the cover grammar conflict between the `void` expression and `await using` declarations, fixed in PR #9 and approved by [WH](../../people/WH.md) — and asked for Stage 2 for [Discard bindings](../../proposals/discard-bindings.md). The `void` vs `_` debate dominated: [DLM](../../people/DLM.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), [LCA](../../people/LCA.md) and [WH](../../people/WH.md) preferred underscore (precedent across other languages, Java now included), while [MF](../../people/MF.md) defended `void` via JavaScript's own void-operator precedent and [SFC](../../people/SFC.md) argued `void` carries unit-type baggage from other languages and that leaving the choice open made this a weak Stage 2 — though he would not block. [WH](../../people/WH.md) did not want both forms as discards.

The conclusion was consensus on Stage 2, with the underscore-vs-void question explicitly postponed to Stage 2.

## Algorithms for Signals

- wiki: [Signals](../../proposals/signals.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-signals)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1-_4KHsG6a3ZLuWlV2zz3dwGk2O9R7keEqkerb97NDYQ/)

[DE](../../people/DE.md) gave a Stage 1 deep-dive on the [Signals](../../proposals/signals.md) algorithms: pull-based evaluation with auto-tracking and topological sorting gives coherent, glitch-free incremental updates, while push-based (observable-style) propagation suffers the diamond dependency problem; computed signals cache and compare on the way out via `equals`. He covered the Watcher API (synchronous dirty notification, `.get()` deferred to scheduled tasks), GC-able unwatched computed signals, and the bidirectional-edge bookkeeping both algorithms need. Discussion was mostly clarifying questions ([MF](../../people/MF.md) on `equals` with mutable objects, [CM](../../people/CM.md) on dynamic dependency sets); [WH](../../people/WH.md) raised a self-reconfiguring graph scenario with a temporary cycle during invalidation to investigate offline.

The conclusion was no advancement ask — [DE](../../people/DE.md) expects well over 12 more months at Stage 1.

## Atomics.pause

- wiki: [Atomics.pause](../../proposals/atomics-pause.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/syg/proposal-atomics-microwait)

[SYG](../../people/SYG.md) asked for Stage 2.7 for [Atomics.pause](../../proposals/atomics-pause.md) (renamed from microwait; still unobservable, timing-only). [WH](../../people/WH.md) and [MF](../../people/MF.md) objected to the spec distinguishing `-0` from `+0` in the iteration-number check (violating a spec-wide invariant), and [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) agreed to remove that step. A longer debate followed on whether the implementation-guidance notes on wait scaling can be normative ([DE](../../people/DE.md), [NRO](../../people/NRO.md), [KM](../../people/KM.md), [RGN](../../people/RGN.md)); [MF](../../people/MF.md) would have preferred Stage 2 given the open editorial question but was not opposed to 2.7, and [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) wants Stage 3 next meeting with only presence/callability tests possible.

The conclusion was consensus for Stage 2.7 with step 1.b removed; the editor group will bring a recommendation on the implementation-guidance notes next meeting.

## Shared Structs discussion around methods

- wiki: [Shared structs](../../proposals/shared-structs.md)
- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-structs)
- slide: [link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1aeXqO6uR_HVuWyciudHGRCd8J12UGhqwygYHl3FOmVc/)

[SYG](../../people/SYG.md) recapped the two mechanisms proposed to give [shared structs](../../proposals/shared-structs.md) methods (no advancement ask): realm-local `[[Prototype]]` objects (like primitive boxing, keyed per struct) and opt-in auto-correlation keying realm-local prototypes by source location. [MM](../../people/MM.md) objected that with public fields, method-bearing shared data cannot encapsulate threading concerns and contradicts the WasmGC coexistence rationale — he would prefer free functions only; [CM](../../people/CM.md) raised asymmetric producer/consumer relationships between realms, and [MAH](../../people/MAH.md) noted private fields would need correlation too. [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) said he cannot make progress without understanding [MM](../../people/MM.md)'s constraints, and warned that if the impasse persists the work may land in the JS Wasm API layer instead of TC39.

The conclusion was no consensus sought — stakeholders continue in the regularly scheduled shared structs working session, with [MM](../../people/MM.md) also suggesting bringing the discussion to TG3.
