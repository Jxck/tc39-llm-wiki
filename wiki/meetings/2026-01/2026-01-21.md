## Composable value-backed accessors for Stage 1

- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/LeaVerou/proposal-composable-accessors)
- slide: [link](https://projects.verou.me/proposal-composable-value-accessors/slides/)

[LVU](../../people/LVU.md) (OpenJS) followed up on the class fields introspection proposal that failed Stage 1 in November 2025 with two problem statements: (1) a high signal-to-noise way to declare public data properties that become part of the class shape, and (2) better DX for common "additive" accessor patterns (validation, normalization, side effects). She sketched a base-plus-layers model whose layers could be built-in [decorators](../../proposals/decorators.md). Pushback was strong: [RBN](../../people/RBN.md) said decorators and auto accessors already cover about 90% of the use cases; [RGN](../../people/RGN.md) called it "too much syntax for a much narrower use"; [KM](../../people/KM.md) warned against syntax hiding the 2-3x cost of accessors; [DLM](../../people/DLM.md), [RBR](../../people/RBR.md), [CM](../../people/CM.md), and [WH](../../people/WH.md) were unconvinced of the problem, while [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) supported statement 1 and [SHS](../../people/SHS.md) statement 2.

The conclusion was to continue the conversation later in the day (see the continuation below); no consensus was called in the morning session.

## Stage 3 Proposal Review (Stage 2/2.7 time permitting)

- proposal: [proposal list](https://github.com/tc39/proposals#stage-3)

PKA (Bloomberg) ran the periodic review, skipping proposals heard from recently. Three were flagged as possibly near Stage 4: Legacy RegExp Features (Firefox has an unlanded implementation, which [MM](../../people/MM.md) welcomed), [Dynamic Code Brand Checks](../../proposals/dynamic-code-brand-checks.md) (shipped in Firefox 135, but [NRO](../../people/NRO.md) said it is not ready yet), and [Atomics.pause](../../proposals/atomics-pause.md) ([KM](../../people/KM.md) may take over from [SYG](../../people/SYG.md)). Among Stage 2 items, function.sent stays ([JHX](../../people/JHX.md) wants to keep it), structs and [isTemplateObject](../../proposals/is-template-object.md) need co-champions, dynamic import host adjustment was judged superseded by dynamic code brand checks, [CDA](../../people/CDA.md) urged [RegExp buffer boundaries](../../proposals/regexp-buffer-boundaries.md) as a security win, and the pipeline operator remains stuck on the topic token.

Action items: PKA will check with [ACE](../../people/ACE.md) on the status of JSON.parseImmutable; [RBN](../../people/RBN.md) will take a new look at RegExp Buffer Boundaries in light of [CDA](../../people/CDA.md)'s security case; [SHS](../../people/SHS.md) will reach out to [KOT](../../people/KOT.md) about withdrawing dynamic import host adjustment; [KM](../../people/KM.md) will check with [SYG](../../people/SYG.md) about Atomics.pause and possibly bring it for Stage 4 next meeting.

## Composable value-backed accessors for Stage 1 (cont.)

- proposal: [proposal](https://github.com/LeaVerou/proposal-composable-accessors)
- slide: [link](https://projects.verou.me/proposal-composable-value-accessors/slides/)

Returning later in the day, [LVU](../../people/LVU.md) recapped the morning's feedback (no new syntax, auto accessors cover value-backed public data properties, strong interest in built-in [decorators](../../proposals/decorators.md), mild interest in alias accessors) and narrowed the proposal to built-in decorators as a low-risk first step, citing [NRO](../../people/NRO.md)'s analysis that 75% of accessor usage is property forwarding. Composable accessors via built-in decorators got support from [SHS](../../people/SHS.md), [RBN](../../people/RBN.md), [PFC](../../people/PFC.md), and [MF](../../people/MF.md). For alias accessors as a separate track, [RBR](../../people/RBR.md) objected that the benefit is too small but withdrew after [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) and [CM](../../people/CM.md) pushed back; [JHX](../../people/JHX.md), [CDA](../../people/CDA.md), and [PFC](../../people/PFC.md) supported it.

The conclusion was Stage 1 for composable accessors via built-in decorators and Stage 1 for alias accessors, with the alias-accessor track proceeding as exploration to validate motivation and design space before any further advancement.
